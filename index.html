♠️WAR♣️

How to Play:

  1. Click the Flip Button.
  2. When clicked, you and the computer will flip your cards.
  3. Whoever flips the card with the highest value wins and puts those cards onto the bottom of their pile.
  4. If both of your cards have the same value, a war initiates.
  5. After clicking "Start War" 3 cards will be put to the side and the fourth card on top will be flipped.
  6. Whoever's card has the highest value, they take all of the cards on the current field.
  7. The person that obtains all of the cards wins!
  8. If a player runs out of cards during a war, they lose!
  9. After 5 minutes, the game will stop and whoever has the most cards wins.
